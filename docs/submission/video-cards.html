Assay

Automatic QA for robot teleoperation data. Every episode gets PASS, DOUBT or REJECT, with the reason and the evidence.

The joints say when.

Joint rules on the CPU find the moment: a regrasp, a cut ending, footage that does not match the joints.

Nemotron says what.

Nemotron 3.5 Super VL on Nebius Token Factory watches that clip and answers one fixed question. Nemotron 3.5 Lightning writes the batch summary; Assay fills in every number.

Results

Measured on public data and on a real QA team's labels

6 of 6

rejections in five public LeRobot datasets (308 episodes, none marked as failed) are unusable recordings.

11 of 12

false alarms cleared by Nemotron 3.5 Super VL watching the clip (8 keyframes: 9 of 12); 2 of 2 real problems confirmed. On 5 rejections, keyframes did better: 5 of 5 vs 3 of 5.

99 of 162

comparable regrasps flagged in RoboMIND, 1,678 episodes rejected by Beijing Humanoid's QA. Rule chosen on another benchmark, not retuned.

36% vs 21%

policy success on robomimic after removing the 45 episodes Assay flags vs 45 random ones (3 seeds, 150 rollouts each). Keeping all 300: 39%.
