Assay

Automatic QA for robot teleoperation data. Every episode gets PASS, DOUBT or REJECT, with the reason and the evidence.

Why

Robot foundation models learn from teleoperation demonstrations

15+

large data-collection sites in China, typically with 100 or more robots each

China Business Journal, cited by Jiazi Guangnian

500 to 1,000

CNY per hour of data at Beijing Humanoid's site

Jiazi Guangnian, 2026-06-26

Manual

QA and labeling still rely largely on people; one site head estimates half of 100 hours ends up usable

Same report; an estimate, not a statistic

Results

Measured on public data and on a real QA team's labels

6 of 6

rejections in five public LeRobot datasets (308 episodes, none marked as failed) are unusable recordings. Of 33 sent to a person: 12 real, 12 false positives, 9 not checked.

99 of 162

comparable regrasps flagged in RoboMIND, 1,678 episodes rejected by Beijing Humanoid's QA. Rule chosen on another benchmark, not retuned.

197 of 201

injected cut endings caught on held-out datasets, with 0 of 202 clean episodes flagged (second look at the test set, disclosed).

What failed

Smoothness metrics did not separate "unsmooth" episodes on real labels. We kept a duration rule that catches 14%, and say so.

Placeholders

It writes {n_doubt}, not 13. A digit it writes itself is rejected; one retry, then a template.
